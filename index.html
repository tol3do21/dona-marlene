About Us

Meet Doña Marlene
& the Cejas Family

We grew up in the fragrant clouds of llajwa and sopa de maní. Our kitchen in Sucre is not a restaurant — it is our home, and you are our guest.

Doña Marlene has been cooking these recipes her whole life. Her daughters guide you in clear, patient Spanish, while Mamá reveals the secrets of our traditional home cooking.
