Why Sucre?

South America's
Spanish Capital

Sucre is widely considered the best city in Latin America to learn Spanish. Bolivians speak a clear, neutral, and friendly Spanish — no strong accents, no confusing local slang — just beautiful, standard Spanish you can use anywhere in the world.

And what better way to practice than in the sunny courtyard with Lorena (Lolo)? Classes are named "Español con Lolo" in her honor, as she leads the teaching. With her, Spanish is not just a subject — it is the language of your daily life.
