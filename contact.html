FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to speak Spanish to join the cooking class?

Not at all! The Cejas daughters are fluent in English and will guide you through every step. Doña Marlene will add the flavor (and the Spanish vocabulary) as you go.

Can I book a cooking class even if I'm not staying for accommodation?

Absolutely. All our classes and services are available independently. You can come for just a cooking class, or just Spanish lessons — no package required. We're flexible!

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend at least 48 hours in advance to guarantee your spot, especially for the popular salteña class. However, if you're already in Sucre and just decided — message us anyway! We'll do our best.

Are dietary restrictions accommodated?

Yes. Please let us know about any allergies, dietary restrictions or food preferences when you write to us, and we will adapt the menu accordingly.
