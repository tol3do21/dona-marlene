Our Homestay Philosophy

Not Just Lodging.
Live with a Bolivian Family.

Our spacious family apartment on Calle Bernardo Bitti #9 is an easy 10–15 minute walk from Plaza 25 de Mayo. Here, you are not renting an anonymous room — you are welcoming yourself into genuine family life.

You sit together with Doña Marlene and her daughters for breakfast and lunch at the family table. You share home-cooked meals, daily laughter, cultural stories, and natural Spanish conversation. That is why travelers from around the world leave so touched and write the beautiful notes in our guestbook — you arrive as a traveler and leave as family.
